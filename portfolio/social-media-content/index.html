Social Media Print

My latest social media postings — a running showcase of the graphics I've been making for Instagram and LinkedIn.

A rotating selection of recent Instagram and LinkedIn posts across Kidashi Design client projects — including Čiganski Delight at Revolution Club Tbilisi, Rohyma Jet, Dotta Eis and Uzu House — concert announcements, story formats and feed graphics designed to carry each project's visual language across both platforms.

The Challenge

Several different client brands — from a jazz concert series to a private jet charter — each needed social content that carried their own visual language consistently across Instagram and LinkedIn, without one generic template flattening them all into the same look.

The Solution

Kidashi Design develops each project's visual concept individually — for Čiganski Delight, for example, balancing jazz tradition with contemporary Tbilisi energy — designs it first at full A1 print scale for real-world testing, then adapts it into feed and story formats for ongoing rollout.

This project combines Kidashi Design's Social Media Management and Print Design services.

“The poster should feel as alive as the performance.”

— Nicole Szatkowski, Kidashi Design

Process & Approach

3 Steps
01

Concert Identity

Visual concept developed for the Čiganski Delight series — balancing jazz tradition with contemporary Tbilisi energy.

02

Poster Design

Full A1 print poster designed for Revolution Club — tested at scale for street-level visual impact.

03

Social Rollout

Feed and story formats adapted from the poster system, published across Instagram and LinkedIn as the campaign continued.
