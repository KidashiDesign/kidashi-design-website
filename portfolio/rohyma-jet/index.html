Services Logo Design, Corporate Identity

Logo & Corporate Identity for a Luxury Private Jet Service in the Gulf Region

Private jet charter headquartered in Abu Dhabi and Dubai. Crafting the logo and corporate identity—from initial concept to a fully realized brand presence, delivered at the pinnacle of premium aviation.

The Challenge

A luxury private jet charter in Abu Dhabi and Dubai needed a logo and corporate identity built entirely from scratch — credible enough for the premium aviation segment from the very first touchpoint.

The Solution

Kidashi Design developed the logomark first, then built out the full corporate identity system — colors, typography, and application rules — and rolled it out consistently across every relevant brand touchpoint.

This project falls under Kidashi Design's Brand Identity work.

“A brand that speaks quietly and lands loudly.”

— Nicole Szatkowski, Kidashi Design

Process & Approach

3 Steps
01

Logo Development

Concept and design of the logomark — crafted for luxury, precision and trust in the private aviation segment.

02

Corporate Identity

Building the complete CI system: colors, typography, application rules — consistent and clear across every brand touchpoint.

03

Brand Rollout

Rolling out the identity across every relevant format — from briefing to the final brand presence.
