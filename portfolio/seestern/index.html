Brand Identity Merchandise Print

A playful brand identity that lives as happily on a t-shirt as on a menu card.

Complete brand identity for a lakeside venue in Berlin's Britzer Garten. Logo with multiple seahorse variations, followed by a full merch rollout — t-shirts, accessories, menu cards and business cards.

The Challenge

A lakeside venue in Berlin's Britzer Garten needed a brand identity playful enough for merchandise people would actually want to wear, but consistent enough to also work on a menu card and business cards.

The Solution

Kidashi Design designed a primary logo plus a set of seahorse variations flexible enough to work across wearables, print, and digital, then extended the system into a full merchandise and print rollout — t-shirts, accessories, a menu card, and business cards, all delivered production-ready.

This project combined Kidashi Design's Brand Identity and Print Design services.

“Brand identity that guests want to take home with them.”

— Nicole Szatkowski, Kidashi Design

Process & Approach

3 Steps
01

Logo System

Primary logo plus seahorse variation set designed to flex across wearables, print and digital.

02

Merchandise

T-shirt graphics and accessory designs — artwork prepared for production with supplier-ready files.

03

Print Collateral

Menu card and business cards designed to match the venue's relaxed, coastal atmosphere.
