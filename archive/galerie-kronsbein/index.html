Galerie Kronsbein

Category Web Design · Brand Identity
Year 2023
Client Galerie Kronsbein
Services Web Design, Logo Redesign, Brand Identity
Web Design Brand Identity

A gallery website as considered as the art it presents.

Full website design for Galerie Kronsbein — an established contemporary art gallery. Seven pages designed with the precision the brand demands: from home and exhibition listings to individual artwork detail and press archive.

“The website should feel like walking into the gallery.”

— Nicole Szatkowski, Kidashi Design

Process & Approach

3 Steps
01

Brand Foundation

Logo refinement, color palette and typographic system established as the base for all digital touchpoints.

02

Page Design

Seven pages designed — home, exhibitions, exhibition detail, gallery view, artwork, interior projects and press archive. Each layout serves the art without competing with it.

03

Delivery

Final designs handed over as production-ready assets with clear structure for development handoff.
