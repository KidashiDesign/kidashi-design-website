A Brand That's Recognizable Everywhere

Brand identity at Kidashi Design means a complete visual system — logo, color palette, typography, and brand guidelines — developed personally by Nicole Szatkowski. A strong brand is more than a logo: it's a consistent system that carries through every touchpoint, making your business unmistakable while giving you a clear rulebook to keep working with confidently.

Logo design, individually developed and tailored to your business

Color palette and typography system, tuned for recognizability and impact

Brand guidelines as a reference document – for you or for teams and external vendors
