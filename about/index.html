About Kidashi Design

Kidashi Design is a media design studio founded by Nicole Szatkowski.

Nicole Szatkowski is an IHK-certified Media Designer specializing in digital and print design, brand identity, web design, and social media design.

Originally from Bavaria, Germany, Nicole currently works from Tbilisi, Georgia and partners with clients internationally.

She works with businesses, startups, and creative projects across a range of industries — from hospitality and travel brands to private aviation. See the full portfolio for examples of that work.

My Path

I completed my formal training at the Mediadesign University of Applied Sciences (MD.H) in Munich, building on three years of hands-on content management experience.

That experience still shapes how I think about design today: it's not just about how something looks, it's about what it communicates and who it's for. After my training, I worked as a graphic designer for several agencies, gaining hands-on experience across multiple disciplines, before going freelance in 2025 to focus fully on the studio.

A Flexible Approach

Working as a digital nomad means I bring a flexible, remote-friendly way of working to every project. I stay easy to reach and personally involved from start to finish, no matter where your business is based.

How I Work

I work well independently, but the best ideas usually come from working closely with my clients, not around them. I bring structure and fast turnarounds, paired with genuine curiosity about your business — so the result doesn't just look good, it actually fits.
